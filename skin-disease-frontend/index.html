What does SkinCare AI do?

  • Analyzes uploaded skin images using advanced AI and deep learning.
  • Detects multiple common skin conditions with confidence guidance.
  • Supports early risk awareness for timely medical decisions.
  • Generates personalized insights based on user health inputs.
  • Provides recommended care steps and safety precautions.
  • Offers educational summaries of symptoms and conditions.
  • Helps track skin health patterns over time.

How does it work?

  • Upload a clear, well-lit image of the affected area.
  • The AI model processes the image and extracts visual features.
  • Patterns are compared against trained disease categories.
  • The system predicts the most likely condition with confidence.
  • Insights, remedies, and precautions are generated for guidance.
  • Consultation prompts appear when follow-up is recommended.
  • Privacy-focused processing limits data use to analysis.

Why use SkinCare AI?

  • Accessible AI-powered screening from any location.
  • Saves time before an in-person consultation.
  • Supports early detection and preventive care decisions.
  • Clear, user-friendly workflow for all users.
  • Confidence-based insights improve decision quality.
  • Educational guidance improves health literacy.
  • Encourages professional care when needed.
